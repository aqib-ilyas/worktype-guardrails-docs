# Privacy Policy

Work Type Guardrails for Jira is published by Aqib Ilyas as an individual.

## What the app stores

The app stores work type IDs. These are the From and To types of each rule you create. They are saved in a project property inside your own Jira site.

That is the only data the app stores. It does not store issue content, user names, email addresses, or any other personal data.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect personal data.
- It does not transmit data outside your Jira site.
- It makes no external network calls. The app runs on Atlassian infrastructure and reads and writes only within your Jira site.

## Data location and removal

The rule data lives in a project property in your Jira site, under your control. Removing a rule on the settings page deletes its stored data. Uninstalling the app removes the app's access.
